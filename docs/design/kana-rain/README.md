# Kana Rain design archive

Approval mockups and explanatory infographics are kept outside the application and its runtime assets. They preserve design context and are not included in the production bundle.

- [Immersive screen concepts](./immersive-v1/PROMPTS.md): four approval mockups from 7 October 2026 and their complete ImageGen prompts. The production art and sprite sources remain in `01-kana-rain/assets/`.
- [Technical and design infographics](./infographics-2026-10-13/prompts-e-fontes.json): four explanatory images from 13 October 2026, their prompts and source notes, plus the [revision instructions](./infographics-2026-10-13/ajuste-imagem-03.txt).

The infographics describe the project at the time they were generated. For current behavior, use the [game README](../../../01-kana-rain/README.md) and [new-kana rules](../../../01-kana-rain/NOVELTY_BALANCE.md).
