+## concept

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: concept art presentation for Kana Rain, an educational browser game about reading falling Japanese kana.
Primary request: Create a polished landscape visual development board with two distinct large illustrated panels: left a quiet Japanese garden in rain viewed from a wooden veranda, right a close view of luminous water drops with little rain spirits and visual effects. This is conceptual art before game implementation.
Style: elegant hand-painted storybook environment, fine ink lines, restrained watercolor washes and atmospheric depth, premium indie game art.
Scene: mossy stones, a small pond, fern edges, Japanese maple, distant roof silhouette in mist, blue-green rain, warm amber lantern glow. Playful turquoise round rain spirit.
Composition: broad uncluttered central sky area in garden panel; lower environmental details. Panels separated cleanly. Detailed enough to guide assets.
Palette: deep midnight teal, desaturated jade, warm ivory, small amber accents.
Constraints: no text, no kana lettering, no UI, no watermark. Keep garden serene and central gameplay space readable.
```
