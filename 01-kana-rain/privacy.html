Privacy and cookies

Updated 8 October 2026. This notice covers Nihongo Grove (nihongogrove.top), Japanese Core (kana.nihongogrove.top), and Kana Rain (kana-rain.nihongogrove.top).

You can use all three sites with analytics off. The Google Analytics library loads only after you choose Allow analytics. Declining sends no analytics requests to Google.

What optional analytics does

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages and apps visitors use and whether practice sessions start and finish. Our own events contain only the app, a fixed practice-mode category, the destination app, or a fixed completion reason.

After you opt in, Google also processes normal analytics information such as a browser identifier, session information, device and browser characteristics, and approximate geographic information derived from the network connection. Your browser connects to Google, so Google receives your IP address during that connection. GA4 does not log or store individual IP addresses. See Google's GA4 data collection information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Page URLs sent by our tracking code exclude query strings and fragments. Only known page or screen names are allowed. Referrers contain only the referring site's origin. We disable Enhanced Measurement, Google signals, advertising storage, and ad personalization. We do not configure advertising integrations.

What we do not send

We do not send typed answers, kana items, scores, accuracy, learner records, saved progress, exports, names, email addresses, usernames, access keys, or account identifiers to Google Analytics. Kana Rain saves progress on this device. Japanese Core supports a functional username and access-key profile and synchronizes its learning progress with its own server. This app storage and synchronization are separate from optional Google Analytics. Turning analytics off does not erase your learning progress.

Your choice applies to this browser across the three sites. A different browser or device has its own choice. We respect Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by keeping analytics off. If the browser blocks the preference cookie, analytics stays off.

Change your mind

Use Analytics settings on any page, then choose Turn analytics off. This stops future measurement and removes the accessible Google Analytics cookies. Open sibling sites check for changes before each app event, on focus and visibility changes, and through a one-second timer (which browsers may throttle in the background). Requests already sent before withdrawal cannot be recalled, and withdrawal does not delete previously collected data from Google's reports.

Limits of measurement

Our reports describe visitors who allow analytics and whose browsers can reach Google. Consent choices, ad blockers, offline use, and network failures make these counts incomplete. Practice completion means the session ended with a known reason; it does not prove learning or mastery. We never replay activity that happened before you allowed analytics.

Nihongo Grove and Kana Rain do not require sign-in. Japanese Core's username and access-key profile provides progress synchronization with the app's own server; it is not a Google Analytics identifier. Clearing all site data may remove device-local progress or a remembered app profile. You can remove cookies through your browser settings and use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
