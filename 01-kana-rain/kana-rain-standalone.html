How to play

Type the romaji of any falling kana (e.g. ka, shi, tsu) — answers submit automatically; press Enter/Space to force-submit (needed for n). A wrong answer makes that drop fall faster and stains it until you solve it. Three drops on the ground and the game ends.

In Pair mode, tap (or press 1–6) the hiragana that matches the falling katakana. New rows unlock as you master the current ones; kana you confuse come back side by side.
