WELCOME TO THE GARDEN

Start with Hiragana and type its sound: あ → a, し → shi. Correct answers submit automatically. Short answers such as n wait briefly for another letter; stop typing to accept, or press Enter immediately.

Choose Hiragana or Katakana and your difficulty on the main menu. Each session begins with a short countdown; choose Skip countdown when you are ready. You can answer any visible drop; the highlighted drop is the priority.

Read the rain and follow your correct answers, mistakes and expired drops. Each accepted correct answer counts once, including corrections; each wrong submission counts once. Expiring a drop is counted separately and does not add another wrong answer. A regular run lasts three minutes, with three hearts. On every difficulty, rain speeds up as you answer correctly and eases off whenever a drop gets through. Every drop that reaches the ground costs one heart and clears any unfinished answer; losing all hearts ends the run.

New kana briefly show their sound. Open My Kana Garden and tap a lesson tile to listen. A new row opens when each available kana has two correct first answers and at least 85% first-answer accuracy. Reading hints and pronunciation do not block unlocks. Wrong answers show the correct reading, stain the drop, and make it fall faster. Missed or mistaken kana return for practice.

Romaji hints disappear as you learn Hiragana. On a touch screen, the sound pad builds its answers in two taps: choose the sound family, then the sound — し → S/SH → shi, しゃ → S/SH → sha, ん → N → n. The pad keeps the same places for every kana; ‹ goes back. The keyboard button switches between the pad and your device keyboard.

Katakana follows Hiragana. New gardens unlock it after all 104 Hiragana have two correct first answers and at least 85% first-answer accuracy. Previously earned Katakana access stays available. In Katakana, choose the matching Hiragana from six options: シ → し, キャ → きゃ. Tap a drop to select it. Click an option or press 1–6, following the displayed numbers. Options stay in place until that drop is answered or expires; no Japanese keyboard or romaji typing is needed. These answers record recognition with choices, separately from independent recall.

An ! badge warns that a drop is close to the ground. After a run, the results name the next row and how many first-try hits each kana still needs. Tap a review card to hear its kana, or choose Review these kana for a focused one-minute round with three hearts. My Kana Garden shows next-row readiness and optional learning details.

Difficulty controls new kana, not rain speed. Any wrong answer or expired drop pauses new introductions until ten consecutive first-try hits on kana you have already met. Corrections and debut hits do not advance this recovery. Two expired drops or four wrong first answers on familiar kana in the recent window also close the longer brake. Easy brings them back with 55 first-try hits in the latest 60 completed drops, Normal with 25 in 30, or Hard with 10 in 12, once fewer than two expired drops and fewer than four of those wrong first answers remain. These are recovery targets after a pause, not requirements before every new kana. Both the recent history and the ten-hit pause continue between sessions.

New kana arrive after three familiar completions, at most two per run and one debut on screen. Each alphabet's five starter vowels bypass the recovery, spacing and two-debut limits. All earned rows remain unlocked.

Esc pauses or returns from a menu. Leaving the tab pauses automatically. Every answer saves on this device.
